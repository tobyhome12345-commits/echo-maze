Ripples reveal the maze — but a monster your ripple hits will go to the spot you sent it from, so move on. If you stay close it can hear your footsteps, and then it follows you until you get away. Touch one and you're dead. Hold Shift to crouchHold CROUCH to crouch: you make no sound and cannot send a ripple, and crouching wipes everything your ripples had shown you. Find the green resonance and get out.
